Account & Data Deletion

This page is for account and data deletion requests for {{name}} (Android, developer: {{developerName}}).

In the game (instant)

  1. Tap your avatar in the top-left corner and open the ACCOUNT plate on the PROFILE screen. (SETTINGS → ACCOUNT AND PRIVACY → GAME ACCOUNT leads to the same place.)
  2. Tap DELETE MY ACCOUNT, then YES, DELETE PERMANENTLY.
  3. Choose the account you signed in with (Google or Facebook) once more to confirm it's you.

Your account and all the data in the table below are deleted immediately, and the game on that phone starts over. This button only appears if you signed in (with Google or Facebook). If you played as a guest, we hold no data about you on our servers; uninstalling the game removes the save on your phone.

If you signed in with Facebook

Deleting your account in the game or with the web form below also deletes all data that came into the game through Facebook sign-in: your name, email address, profile photo address and the Facebook user ID linked to your game account. To also remove Auto Yard's access to your Facebook account, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites in Facebook, find Auto Yard and tap Remove (menu names may differ slightly between Facebook versions). Removing access on its own doesn't delete your game progress; use the steps above for that.

With the web form

If you've uninstalled the game or can't open it:

  1. Fill in the form below with the email address of the Google or Facebook account you signed in with. If your Facebook account has no email address, write your Facebook name in the note field.
  2. We'll send you an email to confirm the request came from that address; reply to it.
  3. After confirmation, the deletion is completed within 30 days at the latest and we'll let you know.

If you can't use the form, write to {{contactEmail}} with the same details.
