Account & Data Deletion

This page is for account and data deletion requests for {{name}} (Android, developer: {{developerName}}).

In the game (instant)

  1. Tap your avatar in the top-left corner and open the ACCOUNT plate on the PROFILE screen. (SETTINGS → ACCOUNT AND PRIVACY → GOOGLE ACCOUNT leads to the same place.)
  2. Tap DELETE MY ACCOUNT, then YES, DELETE PERMANENTLY.
  3. Choose the same Google account once more to confirm it's you.

Your account and all the data in the table below are deleted immediately, and the game on that phone starts over. This button only appears if you signed in with Google. If you played as a guest, we hold no data about you on our servers; uninstalling the game removes the save on your phone.

With the web form

If you've uninstalled the game or can't open it:

  1. Fill in the form below with the email address of the Google account you signed in with.
  2. We'll send you an email to confirm the request came from that address; reply to it.
  3. After confirmation, the deletion is completed within 30 days at the latest and we'll let you know.

If you can't use the form, write to {{contactEmail}} with the same details.

Files on your phone stay only on your device; to remove them, uninstall the game or go to Settings → Apps → {{name}} → Storage → Clear data.
