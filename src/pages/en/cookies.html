How does Vercel Web Analytics work?

If you agree, information such as page views, the page visited, the referring site, country and device type is counted in aggregate. No cookie or persistent ID that identifies you is created, and you are not tracked across sites. Details: Vercel Analytics privacy information.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a rejection and don't load the statistics script.

Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time. If you withdraw consent, the statistics script is no longer loaded from the next page view.
