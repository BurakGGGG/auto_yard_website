What can you do?

Way more than a garage

A repair shop, a junkyard, a tuning workshop, a drag strip and your friends: all in one city.

  • Repair shop

    Customers come straight from traffic

    A broken-down car pulls over; you tap it and put it on a lift. Engine, brakes, tires, bodywork, upholstery… When the job is done, tap the ₺ bubble to collect. Waiting customers get impatient: leave them too long and they honk and drive off!

    • 7 job types, from quick tire swaps to brake overhauls
    • 3 movable lifts, 4 garage sizes and repair-speed upgrades
    • 10 garage ranks and 99 levels

  • Junkyard

    From rust to legend

    Rusty classics are waiting at the junkyard on the map. Dig through the piles, collect parts from bolts to pistons and ask a friend for the one you're missing. The wreck on your lift turns into a shiny classic, stage by stage.

    • 28 classics, from the Hitit A-67 to the Bull Matador 400
    • 3 new cars every day, piles that refill every 8 hours
    • A separate map with Necati, his apprentice Cemo and its own music

  • Drag strip

    Nail the launch, shift on time

    No steering on the 300 m strip, just skill: launch the moment the lights turn green and shift when the needle hits the green zone. Rivals are matched to your car, and if you lose you get one rematch.

    • A real gearbox model with perfect launches and shifts
    • A personal record for every car and a ghost of your record pace
    • Most races are against your friends' tuned cars

  • Tuning

    Take your car up a class

    Engine, turbo, airflow, gearbox, tires and body: 30 parts for every car. The dyno shows the before/after difference right away, and the right parts move your car up to the next class.

    • 6 categories, 5 tiers from Street to Legend
    • Carburettors for classics, battery parts for EVs
    • Live dyno with a power curve

  • Decoration

    Design the garage of your dreams

    Paint the floor tile by tile, build interior walls, hang signs and posters. A jacuzzi, a giant aquarium, even a private helicopter… Show off your cars in the garage for visiting friends to see.

    • 263 items: workshop, lounge, yard, plants, luxury
    • Tile-by-tile floor painting, interior walls and wall coverings
    • Decor also raises your Garage Value

  • Friends

    Race your friends, visit their garages

    Add friends with a code or a one-tap invite link. Visit their garages and send them the junkyard part they need. Most rivals show up in your friends' tuned cars, and if one beats you, you'll hear about it: GET REVENGE!

    • Invite link: whoever opens it adds you right away
    • Head-to-head score, win rate and collection on every card
    • Master Emre's and Master Elif's garages are open to everyone

  • Collection

    Open crates, complete your collection

    City, family, sport, prestige and super crates are delivered to the showroom. The odds for every car are shown in the shop, and finding a car again adds a star. Your first car is a gift: the Bursan Atmaca in the welcome box.

    • 5 crates, 4 rarities: common, rare, epic, legendary
    • Odds are shown up front, no surprises
    • Every car you discover for the first time earns gems

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Auto Yard free?

Yes, it's free to download and play. You can buy optional gem packs on Google Play, but gems are also earned in the game (when you level up, on daily login and from tasks), so you never have to buy anything.

Are there ads in the game?

Only optional rewarded ads. An ad opens only when you tap WATCH AD; in return your race prize is doubled or the remaining time of a long repair is cut in half. There are no interstitial or banner ads, ads never give gems, and you can watch at most 12 ads a day.

Which phones can run it?

Android 7.0 or newer, on 64-bit (arm64) phones. The game picks the graphics quality for your phone automatically; you can change it in SETTINGS and turn on battery saver.

Do I need an account?

No, you can play as a guest. If you sign in with Google, Facebook or an email code, your progress is backed up to the cloud and the friend features (invites, garage visits, friend races) unlock.

How do I play with friends?

Sign in with Google, Facebook or an email code and pick a nickname. On the FRIENDS screen, tap SEND INVITE LINK or enter your friend's code. A friend who opens your invite link adds you straight away; the link is valid for 1 hour. Then you can visit each other's garages and race each other's cars.

Do I know what's inside a crate?

Yes. The cars in each crate and their odds are listed next to the crate in the shop. Finding a car again adds a star, and every car you discover for the first time earns gems.

Which languages does the game support?

English, Spanish, German, French and Turkish. The game starts in your phone's language, and you can change it in SETTINGS at any time.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the game, go to PROFILE → ACCOUNT → DELETE MY ACCOUNT (SETTINGS → ACCOUNT AND PRIVACY → GAME ACCOUNT leads to the same place). If you can't open the game, use the form on the Account & Data Deletion page.

When is it coming out?

Very soon on Google Play. To hear about it on launch day, choose "Notify me when the game launches" in the contact form.
