2. Data processed in the game

When you play as a guest

No account is created and no Firebase session is started for guest play. Your save (money, cars, garage, tasks, settings), your purchase ledger and your daily ad counters are stored only on your phone. Your phone's graphics chip, memory and processor details are used only on the device to choose the graphics quality and are never sent anywhere. Guests can still watch rewarded ads and make purchases; see sections 4 and 5.

When you sign in (Google, Facebook or email)

  • Account: Google Firebase Authentication signs you in with the Google or Facebook account you choose and gives you a user ID (UID). You can also sign in with a one-time code sent to your email address (see below). Your Google account, Facebook account and email sign-in with the same email address are combined into one game account, so any of them signs you in to the same account. No other sign-in method is used.
  • Profile details: your Google or Facebook display name, email address and profile photo address (URL) are kept in a part of your cloud save that only you can read. Your display name is only shown on your own screen; other players see the nickname you choose.
  • Facebook sign-in: if you choose Facebook, the Facebook app or website opens and you approve sharing only your public profile (name, profile photo) and email address with the game; your Facebook password never reaches the game. Firebase Authentication signs you in by verifying the login token Facebook issues and links an app-specific Facebook user ID to your game account; the token is not written to your cloud save. Your friends list and posts are not accessed, and nothing is posted on your behalf. Your Facebook profile photo is not used in the game. The Facebook component in the game (Facebook SDK) is used only for sign-in; automatic app events and advertising ID collection are turned off. Facebook processes the sign-in data under its own privacy policy.
  • Email code sign-in: if you choose email, we send a 6-digit, single-use sign-in code to the address you enter (email delivery service: Resend); no password is used. Our server (Google Cloud Functions) keeps the code only as a hash; it is valid for 10 minutes and stops working once used. When the code is verified, Firebase Authentication creates an account for you with that email address; if you already have a Google or Facebook account with that address, you sign in to that account. To limit abuse (for example, many codes sent to someone else's address), a hash of your email address and of the requesting IP address, together with sending times, is kept for at most 1 hour and then deleted automatically.
  • Cloud save: your whole game progress (economy, cars, garage and decor, tasks, purchase ledger, the list of players you blocked, race counts with your friends, your recent race runs and your personal race-difficulty counts) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, linked to your account and readable only by your account.

When you use the friend features

The friend features require signing in (with Google, Facebook or email) and a nickname. When you create your nickname:

  • Your public garage profile is created: your nickname, friend code, level, garage value, garage layout (decor, displayed cars and their tuning), avatar, frame, the flag you choose, garage name, showcase car, race stats (wins, races, collection, best time) your recent race runs (the last 8 per car: the moments of the launch and each gear shift, the car's tuning and the time; in your friends' races your car drives one of these runs) and the junkyard part you asked for, if any. Your money, gems and crates are not part of it.
  • Profile photo: a photo you choose from your gallery, take with the camera or your Google account photo is resized to 192×192 pixels and uploaded to Firestore. Your Google photo only becomes your avatar if you choose GOOGLE or turn on GOOGLE: ON under EDIT PROFILE → PHOTO; it is off by default. When you remove your photo or switch to a preset avatar, the uploaded image is deleted from the server.
  • Friendship data: your friends list, friend requests (nickname, code and time), the junkyard parts you send to friends as gifts, race news (nickname, the cars raced and the result) and invite links (your ID, nickname and code; a link is valid for 1 hour and an expired invite is deleted within an hour).
  • Reports and blocking: when you report a player, the reason, the reported player's ID and nickname and, for photo reports, a copy of the photo are stored; only the developer reviews reports, and they are deleted automatically after 180 days. The list of players you block is kept in your own save, and the blocked player is not notified.

Race notifications (optional)

Only you can turn notifications on (you're asked after your first race, or in SETTINGS → SOUND AND NOTIFICATIONS). When you do, your phone's notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and your language are stored. A notification is sent when a friend beats your car and contains their nickname, at most once every 6 hours per friend. To limit how often notifications are sent, the server records the two player IDs and the time of sending; this record is deleted automatically after 1 day. Turning notifications off deletes the token.

Race log and rival selection

When you are signed in, a short summary of every race you play is saved in Firestore, linked to your account: when it happened, the car you used and the car you faced, both times, the result, the difficulty level the rival was chosen at and your chance of winning, your player level, the type of rival (friend, featured mechanic or unnamed) and whether, in the few minutes after the race, you started another race or closed the game. Every night an anonymous summary table is built from all players' logs (after which race difficulty, in which situation, how many players kept playing). Using this table and your own race history, the game chooses which of the rival's real runs you face next; the result of a race is never changed, and the rival always drives either a friend's real run or a run the game drove under the same rules. Other players cannot see the log; only the developer can access it. It is deleted automatically after 180 days. When you play as a guest, the log and your difficulty counts stay on your phone.

Usage statistics and error reports

To improve the game and fix bugs we use Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics counts which screens you open and what you do in the game (e.g. repairs, races, opening crates, levelling up, the result of a purchase) using a random app ID specific to your device; it is not linked to your account, name or email address. When the game crashes or hits an error, Crashlytics sends where it happened, your device model, Android and app version, and the screens that were open just before; if you're signed in, your user ID (UID) is added to the report so we can help you. The advertising ID is not collected for this, and the data is not used for advertising. You can turn both off at any time in SETTINGS → ACCOUNT AND PRIVACY (USAGE STATISTICS, ERROR REPORTS); once off, nothing is sent from that device.

Technical data

To work, Firebase services process technical data such as IP address, device and app version, and an app installation ID. Details: Firebase privacy information.

What the game does not do

The game does not access your location, contacts or microphone, and the race log, usage statistics and error reports are not used for advertising or profiling. Your data is never sold.

3. What other players can see

Your public garage profile and profile photo can be seen by players signed in to Auto Yard (when they visit your garage, in their friends list, when they race against you and when they open your invite link). Friend requests and race news are seen only by the two players involved. Your cloud save, email address and Google or Facebook display name are never shown to other players.

4. Ads

The game only shows optional rewarded ads. An ad opens only when you tap WATCH AD (to double a race prize or shorten a long repair); there are no interstitial, banner or app-open ads.

Ads are served through Appodeal. For each ad, Appodeal runs an auction between connected ad networks (AppLovin, Unity Ads, Mintegral, Liftoff/Vungle, Meta Audience Network, Moloco, IronSource/LevelPlay, Pangle, InMobi, Digital Turbine, BidMachine). Appodeal and the network that shows the ad may process your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (e.g. model, operating system, language, connection type) and your interactions with ads in order to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form is shown when the game starts; you can change your choice later in SETTINGS → ACCOUNT AND PRIVACY → AD PRIVACY OPTIONS. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads. The game does not use child-directed ad settings and does not request location permission for ads. Details: Appodeal privacy policy.

5. In-app purchases

Gem packs are sold through Google Play. Your payment details (card etc.) never reach us; Google processes the payment. So that a pack is never granted twice and can be restored if lost, a purchase ledger keeps the product, the number of gems, the time and the Google order number (the purchase token only until the transaction is complete). The ledger is part of your save, so if you're signed in it's also in your cloud save. Your phone also keeps one-way hashes of purchase tokens. To check that a purchase is genuine, the purchase token, product and package name are sent to our server (Google Cloud Functions), which asks Google Play; gems are only granted if Google confirms the purchase. The server keeps a one-way hash of the token, the product, order number, quantity, verification result and time, and your user ID (UID) if you're signed in, for 1 year.

6. Permissions, camera and photos

  • Camera: requested only when you tap CAMERA to take a profile photo, and used only while that window is open.
  • Gallery: you pick a profile photo with Android's own file picker; the game only sees the file you choose and does not ask for gallery access.
  • Notifications: on Android 13 and newer, requested only when you turn race notifications on.
  • Photo mode: the game photos you take are saved to your phone's Pictures/Auto Yard folder and are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Internet: for cloud save, friend features, ads and purchases.

7. Data processed on the website

  • Server logs: the Site is hosted on Vercel. For security and operations, Vercel briefly logs data such as IP address, browser details and the requested page.
  • Anonymous visit statistics: Vercel Web Analytics is loaded only if you agree in the cookie settings. It uses no cookies and does not track you across sites.
  • Form data: the name, email and message you enter in the contact or account deletion form are forwarded to us by email (email service: Resend). Your IP address is briefly held in memory to limit abuse.
  • Invite links: the invite page reads the code in the link only in your browser and opens the game; the code is not stored on the server.
  • Preference storage: your cookie choice is stored in your browser's local storage (localStorage) and is not sent to the server.

9. Service providers and international transfers

Your data is shared only with the following processors, and only to provide the service:

  • Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd. (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging): sign-in, cloud save, friend features, notifications, race log
  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics): usage statistics and error reports
  • Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. / Meta Platforms, Inc. (Facebook Login): sign-in, if you choose Facebook
  • Appodeal, Inc. and the ad networks it mediates (AppLovin, Unity Ads, Mintegral, Liftoff/Vungle, Meta, Moloco, IronSource, Pangle, InMobi, Digital Turbine, BidMachine): serving and measuring rewarded ads
  • Google (Google Play): in-app purchases and their verification
  • Vercel Inc.: website hosting and anonymous statistics
  • Resend: forwarding form messages and sending in-game sign-in codes by email

The game's cloud data is stored in Google's European data centres; providers may also process data in other countries, including the US. Transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses and the safeguards in Article 9 of the KVKK. Apart from cases required by law, your data is not shared with anyone else.

10. Retention

  • Cloud save, Google / Facebook profile details, public garage profile, friend code and friends list: until you delete your account. Profile photo: as long as you use it; deleted when you remove it or delete your account.
  • Friend requests: until accepted, declined, withdrawn or the account is deleted.
  • Invite links: valid for 1 hour; an expired invite is deleted automatically within an hour.
  • Email sign-in code: valid for 10 minutes and single-use; the code hash and the sending-limit records (email and IP address hashes) are deleted automatically within 1 hour.
  • Gifted parts: until the recipient collects them. Race news: until dismissed or replaced by the same friend's next race.
  • Notification token: until you turn notifications off or delete your account. Notification sending log: 1 day. Report records: 180 days (for review and preventing abuse).
  • Race log: 180 days, then deleted automatically; anonymous summary tables built from it (which cannot be linked to anyone) may be kept indefinitely. Your recent race runs and personal difficulty counts are part of your cloud save and garage profile: until you delete your account (new runs replace older ones).
  • Data on your phone (save, photos, cache): until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Support emails: up to 1 year after the request is closed. Deletion request records: 3 years as proof (email and request date only).
  • Database backups: taken automatically every day to protect against data loss and kept for 7 days; deleted data also drops out of the backups within 7 days at the latest. Backups are only used to restore data after a failure.
  • Usage statistics: event details are kept in Google Analytics for 2 months, then deleted automatically (not linked to your account). Error reports: 90 days. Purchase verification records: 1 year, then deleted automatically (immediately if you delete your account).
  • Ad data: for the retention periods of Appodeal and the ad networks. Purchase data: for Google's own retention periods. Site server logs: for Vercel's retention periods (usually a few days).

11. Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account in the game via PROFILE → ACCOUNT → DELETE MY ACCOUNT (SETTINGS → ACCOUNT AND PRIVACY → GAME ACCOUNT leads to the same place). This deletes your cloud save, public garage profile, profile photo, friend code, friendships (on both sides), requests, race news, gifts, invites, race log, notification token and your Google / Facebook sign-in links. If you signed in with an email code, you confirm the deletion with a new code sent to your email. If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove Auto Yard's access in Facebook (how?). If you can't open the game, use the form on the Account & Data Deletion page.

After deletion, the following may remain for a while: reports made by you or about you (up to 180 days), notification sending logs (up to 1 day), copies in database backups (up to 7 days), purchase records held by Google, ad records held by Appodeal and the ad networks, and files on your phone (removed when you uninstall the app). Usage statistics are not linked to your account, so they are not deleted with it; they are deleted automatically within 2 months. Error reports remain for up to 90 days. Your purchase verification records are deleted with your account.

12. Security

Cloud data is protected by Firebase security rules: each player can only read and write their own save, and public profiles are only open to signed-in players. The Site and the game's server connections use HTTPS only. No system is perfect; if a breach occurs, we will notify as required by the KVKK and the GDPR.
